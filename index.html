iPhone + Apple Watch

Sweat Builder.

Interval workouts and training plans for people who like to plan their own training. Build the workouts you want, put them into plans, and follow them week by week.

  • iPhone
  • Apple Watch
  • Apple Health
  • iCloud

What it does

  • Build your workouts

    Rounds, supersets, circuits, AMRAPs and EMOMs. Intervals are timed, reps, reps within a time, or open until you tap Done.

  • Describe it

    Say what you want, like “30 min lower body with dumbbells”, and Apple Intelligence writes it into the builder, on your iPhone.

  • Follow a plan

    Put workouts together into multi-week plans or import a plan file. Join part-way, pause, and queue the next plan.

  • One calendar

    Every week, past and coming, in one scrolling list. Add one-off workouts to any day and keep the Calendar app in step.

  • On your wrist

    Run workouts on Apple Watch with haptics, heart rate and energy, and follow along live on the iPhone.

  • Widgets

    Complications, Smart Stack widgets and a Live Activity on the lock screen while a workout runs.

  • Your exercises

    Demo photos, YouTube videos, notes and the equipment each one needs, linked from every workout that uses it.

  • Health & Strava

    Finished workouts are saved to Health and Fitness, and can go to Strava with their heart rate and energy.

  • Share & sync

    Send a plan or workout as a file over AirDrop or Messages. Your library syncs between your devices with iCloud.

  • Shop

    Optional plans and workout and exercise packs, bought once and shared with your family.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
