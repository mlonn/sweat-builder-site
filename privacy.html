Sweat Builder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Sweat Builder is an iPhone and Apple Watch app for interval workouts and training plans. It is made by Mikael Lönn. The short version: the app collects no data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

What stays on your devices

Your workouts, exercises, plans, photos, schedule and history are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch. If you use iCloud, they are also kept in your own iCloud Drive so they sync between your devices. That copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and the developer cannot read it.

Health

With your permission, Sweat Builder saves the workouts you finish to Apple Health and Fitness, and reads your workouts, heart rate, energy and effort back to show them with the workouts you've done. Health data never leaves your devices through the app, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone. You can change what the app may read or write at any time in the Settings app under Health.

Calendar

If you choose to, the app adds your scheduled workouts to a "Sweat Builder" calendar in the Calendar app. It only writes to that calendar.

Describing a workout

Workouts and exercises you describe in words are written by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. The description is not sent to the developer.

YouTube videos

Exercises and workouts can show YouTube videos that you add. They play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced (youtube-nocookie.com) player and their thumbnails load from YouTube, so YouTube receives the request when you view them. Google's privacy policy covers that.

Purchases

Packs in the shop are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer only learns from the App Store that a pack was bought, not who bought it.

Sharing files

Plans and workouts you share are files you send yourself, through the share sheet, to whoever you choose.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
